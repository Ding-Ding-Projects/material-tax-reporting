# Material Tax Reporting brand mark

This folder contains the original app mark assets for Material Tax Reporting.

## Files

- `source-chroma.png` is the reproducible source image on a flat `#ff00ff` background.
- `material-tax-reporting-mark.png` is the transparent PNG produced from `source-chroma.png`.

## Generation mode

The mark was created with the built-in image generation tool, then prepared for transparency by compositing the generated alpha image over a flat `#ff00ff` chroma-key background and running the installed `remove_chroma_key.py` helper with border auto-key detection, soft matte, and despill.

## Non-affiliation boundary

This mark is an original, unofficial application icon. It deliberately avoids a maple leaf, Canadian flag, CRA or Government of Canada wordmark, coat of arms, government form reproduction, currency symbol, tax agency seal, and any other visual cue suggesting official affiliation.

## Final prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square app mark source raster for transparent app icon processing
Primary request: Create one original, text-free, vector-friendly raster app mark for a tax reporting application.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for background removal. The background must be one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, lighting variation, or magenta in the subject.
Subject: a warm, approachable abstract folded paper form combined with a clear human-review check motif and subtle arithmetic/grid geometry. The form should feel like a trustworthy review workflow, not a government form. Use geometric planes and rounded expressive shapes that remain recognizable at 16px and polished at 256px.
Style/medium: clean Material Design 3 expressive app icon mark; simplified, vector-friendly raster illustration; crisp edges; no text.
Composition/framing: centered square composition with generous transparent-safe padding, strong silhouette, balanced icon proportions, no cropped subject.
Lighting/mood: friendly, calm, professional, modern; minimal internal highlights only, no cast shadow or contact shadow.
Color palette: deep blue, teal, and warm amber subject colors only; absolutely no magenta/pink/purple in the subject.
Materials/textures: smooth flat-to-subtle layered paper shapes, simple geometry, no photorealism.
Text (verbatim): none. Do not include letters, words, numerals, symbols, or watermark.
Constraints: high legibility at 16px and 256px; subject fully separated from background with crisp antialiased edges; background exactly flat #ff00ff; no shadows requiring a background.
Avoid: maple leaf, Canadian flag, CRA, Government of Canada wordmark, coat of arms, government form reproduction, currency symbol, tax agency seal, official affiliation cues, words, letters, numbers, watermark, people, faces, photorealistic paper, magenta subject pixels.
```
