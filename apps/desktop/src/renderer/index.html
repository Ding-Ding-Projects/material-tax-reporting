      How this works

      You answer in plain language

      The guided return asks one question at a time, explains why it matters, and points to where the answer can be found.

      Every change is recorded locally

      Encrypted snapshots are committed to an isolated local repository with no remote. Restores, undo, and redo append new revisions.

      You review before export

      Export and print remain locked until you acknowledge the form, calculation, attachment, mailing-address, and signature checks.

      You mail the package yourself

      There is no electronic filing or direct transmission. The final reviewed PDF package is intended for printing and mailing only.
